Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any charges or credits.

Is there a free trial available?

Absolutely! We offer a 14-day free trial on all plans. No credit card required to start your trial.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for Enterprise plans.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your account will remain active until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer discounts for annual plans?

Yes! Annual plans come with a 20% discount compared to monthly billing. You'll save significantly over the year.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption and are fully GDPR compliant. Your data is encrypted both in transit and at rest.
